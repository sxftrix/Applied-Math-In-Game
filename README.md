## Gameplay Overview

You control a player moving between **three lanes**.  
Obstacles slide toward you from the top of the screen — if you fail to dodge, you lose HP.  
The game ends once your HP hits **0**.

---

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|---------|
| **A** / **←** | Move Left |
| **D** / **→** | Move Right |
| **Space** | Jump |

---

## Goal

Avoid every obstacle for as long as you can.  
Every hit reduces your HP bar.  

---

## Game Over

When your HP reaches zero:
- The game pauses automatically  
- The Game Over panel appears  

---

## Core Scripts

- `GameCore.cs` → Handles score, HP bar, and Game Over logic  
- `PlayerCore.cs` → Manages player movement, jumping, and damage  
- `ObstacleCore.cs` → Spawns and moves incoming obstacles
